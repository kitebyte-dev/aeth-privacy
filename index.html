PRIVACY POLICY · LAST UPDATED 18 NOVEMBER 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game Aeth: Color Sort Puzzle Game (the "App") uses. The App is published by Kitebyte ("we"). It applies to the Android version of the App and to the web version.

What we do not collect

We do not collect your email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location. The App has no account system. When you first play, the App asks for a display name so it can greet you; this name is saved only on your device and is never sent to us or to anyone else.

Game progress

Your display name, levels, coins, settings and unlocked cities are saved only on your device. We do not receive this data. Uninstalling the App or choosing Settings → Reset progress deletes it.

Advertising

The App is free and is supported by ads from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, AdMob may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID,
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • device and app information, such as the device model, operating system and language,
  • information about how you interact with ads.

Google's use of this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices: If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the App asks for your consent before showing personalised ads. You can opt out of personalised ads at any time in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads), and you can reset or delete your advertising ID there.

Children

The App is a general-audience puzzle game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not collect personal information from anyone, including children. The display name and game progress stay only on the device and never reach us. To remove them, open Settings → Reset progress in the App or uninstall it. Ad data is handled by Google as described above. You can reset the device's advertising ID, or turn off personalised ads, in the device settings.

Security

Your display name and game progress are stored only on your device, in the App's private storage. Other apps cannot read it, and it is never uploaded, so there is no copy of it on any server that could be lost or leaked.

The App has no login, no passwords and no payments, so we never handle your account or card details.

Ad requests go to Google over encrypted (HTTPS) connections, and Google protects that data under its own security practices, described in the Google Privacy Policy.

No method of storing or sending data is completely secure. If we ever learn of a problem that affects you, we will fix it and update this policy.

Changes

We may update this policy when the App changes. The date at the top will always show the latest version.
